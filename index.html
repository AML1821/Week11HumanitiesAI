Should I Use AI Here?

A decision game for ENC 1102. Work through real writing-course moments, decide whether and how to use generative AI, and reflect on why.

  • Current
    Version 2: your choices follow you. Earlier decisions show up in later scenes, three consequence scenes appear only on certain paths, and the end screen shows the path you took and the roads not taken.
  • Earlier version
    Version 1: six scenarios, feedback on every choice, guided reflections and GenAI log practice, saved in your browser, exportable for submission.
